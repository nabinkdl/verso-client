# Verso User Guide

Verso is a mobile-style social app for sharing posts and short-form reels, joining communities called **Pages**, messaging people, and buying or selling items in the **Marketplace**.

This guide is written for people using the app. It explains where each feature lives and how to complete common tasks.

> **Demo note:** This build uses sample data. You can sign in with any email and password. Most changes are kept for the current browser session and reset when you reload the page. The cart starts with a few sample items, and your light/dark theme choice is remembered.

## 1. Getting started

### Sign in

1. Open Verso.
2. Enter your email or username.
3. Enter your password.
4. Select **Sign In**.

In this demo, any email and password will sign you in.

### Create an account

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Don't have an account? Sign Up**.
2. Enter your **Full Name**, **Username**, **Email Address**, and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign Up**.

You are signed in automatically after creating an account.

Your username is how other people find you in Search. It can contain letters, numbers, dots, and underscores, and it must be unique.

### Reset your password

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select **Send Reset Link**.
4. Follow the instructions shown on the next screen.

If you entered the wrong address, select **Try Another Email**.

### Sign out

1. Open your profile from the bottom navigation.
2. Select **Logout**.

## 2. Finding your way around

The bottom navigation has five tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your main feed of posts, with search, notifications, and post creation |
| **Pages** | Your communities, page discovery, join requests, and the Marketplace |
| **Reels** | A full-screen, scrollable feed of short-form content |
| **Chat** | Your conversations and direct messages |
| **Profile** | Your posts, saved posts, followers, and account settings |

The **Marketplace** is opened from the Pages screen. It does not have its own bottom tab.

Use the **Light / Dark** button at the top-right of the app window to switch themes.

---

## 3. Home feed

The Home feed shows posts from the Verso community. Scroll down to load more posts.

At the top of Home you can:

- **Search** for people.
- **Create a post** with the square plus icon.
- **Open notifications** with the bell icon.

Each post has these actions:

- **Like** — tap the heart. Tap it again to remove your like.
- **Comment** — open the comment panel, read comments, and add your own.
- **Save** — tap the bookmark. Saved posts appear in the **Saved** tab on your profile.

To edit or delete one of your own posts:

1. Select the three-dot menu on the post.
2. Choose **Edit post** or **Delete post**.

To edit or delete your own comment, open the comments and use the options beside your comment.

---

## 4. Creating a post

1. Select the **Create post** icon at the top of Home, or create a post from inside a Page.
2. Select the large media area to add a photo or video.
3. Write a caption.
4. Select **Publish post**.

Before publishing, you can select **remove media** to remove the attachment. Select the **X** at the top to close the composer without posting.

When you create a post from inside a Page, the composer shows **Posting in [Page name]**, and the post appears in that Page's feed.

---

## 5. Reels

Open **Reels** from the bottom navigation to view short-form posts in a full-screen feed.

- Scroll vertically to move between reels.
- Tap the heart to like a reel.
- Tap the comment icon to read and add comments.
- Tap the bookmark to save or unsave a reel.

Reels use the most recent posts and show a mix of videos and images.

---

## 6. Searching and following people

1. Select the **Search** icon at the top of Home.
2. Type a username in the search field.
3. Select **Follow** beside a person to follow them, or **Unfollow** if you already follow them.
4. Tap a person's card to open their profile.

Search matches usernames only. To stop seeing someone's updates, use **Unfollow** in Search.

---

## 7. Notifications

Select the **bell** icon at the top of Home to open notifications.

Notifications cover recent activity such as:

- Likes on your posts
- Comments
- New followers
- New posts
- New reels

If there is no recent activity, the screen shows **no notifications yet**.

## 8. Messages

Open **Chat** from the bottom navigation to see your conversations.

### Read and reply

1. Select a conversation.
2. Type your message.
3. Select the send button or press **Enter**.

Use the back arrow to return to the conversation list.

### Start a conversation

You can start a message from:

- The **Message** button on another person's profile.
- The **Message** button on a Marketplace listing.

When you message a seller from Marketplace, you return to Marketplace after leaving the conversation.

## 9. Pages and communities

Pages are communities with their own feed, members, events, and settings.

Open **Pages** from the bottom navigation.

### Pages tabs

- **My Pages** — pages you belong to. Each card shows your role.
- **Discover** — browse categories and search for communities to join.
- **Requests** — pending join requests for pages you manage.

The **Marketplace** card appears at the top of the Pages screen. A red badge on the Marketplace icon shows how many items are in your cart.

### Join a page

1. Open **Discover**.
2. Choose a category or search for a page.
3. Select **Join Page**.
4. If the page requires approval, the button changes to **Requested** until an admin responds.

Private pages are invite-only.

### Create a page

1. Select the **plus** icon at the top of Pages.
2. On the first step, enter:
   - **Page Name**
   - **Category**
   - **Description**
3. Select **Next**.
4. Choose a visibility option:
   - **Public** — anyone can view and join.
   - **Restricted** — members need approval to post.
   - **Private** — only approved members can see content.
5. Add **Community Rules**, one rule per line.
6. Select **Create Page**.

You become the page admin, and the new page opens automatically.

### Inside a page

What you can see depends on your role:

- **Visitors** see **Feed**, **Events**, and **About**.
- **Members** also see **Members**.
- **Admins and moderators** can access **Manage Team**, **Insights**, and **Settings**.
- **Editors** can create page posts and events.

Common page actions:

- **Feed** — read page posts. Members can create a post from the feed. You can like, comment, and save posts.
- **Events** — see upcoming events. Admins and editors can add an event with a title, date, and description.
- **Members / Manage Team** — view members, filter by role, invite people, review join requests, change roles and permissions, or remove members.
- **About** — read the page description, community rules, category, visibility, and member count.
- **Insights** — view page activity and growth.
- **Settings** — update the page name, category, description, visibility, and rules, then select **Save Changes**.

### Page options

Select the three-dot menu inside a page to:

- **Leave Page** (if you are a member).
- **Report Page**.
- **Request Verification** (page admins, for unverified pages).

Pending join requests are shown with a red badge in the Pages list and a dot beside the **Manage Team** tab.

## 10. Marketplace

Open **Marketplace** from the Pages screen.

The red badge on the Marketplace card shows the total number of items in your cart. It displays **9+** when the count is higher than nine.

### Browse and search

- Choose a category at the top.
- Type in **Search marketplace...** to search by title or description.
- Select the filter icon to filter by **Condition** and **Max price**.
- Select a listing to open its details.

### View a listing

The details screen shows the price, title, category, condition, location, delivery method, description, and seller.

From here you can:

- **Message** the seller.
- **Add to Cart**.
- **Buy Now** to add the item and go straight to the cart.

If the listing is yours, you can **Edit** or **Remove** it instead.

### Cart and checkout

1. Select the cart icon in the Marketplace header.
2. Adjust quantities with **+** and **−**, or remove an item.
3. Select **Clear** to empty the cart.
4. Choose a delivery method:
   - **Local Meet-up** — arrange the location and time with the seller after ordering.
   - **Delivery** — enter a delivery address.
5. Select **Place Order** or **Request Meet-up**.

The subtotal updates as you change quantities.

### Order history

Select the clock icon in the Marketplace header to open **Order History**.

- Select an order to see its status and timeline.
- Delivery orders show estimated delivery information.
- Meet-up orders show the meet-up status.
- Select **Cancel Order** while the order is still in an early state.

### Sell an item

1. Select **+ Sell Item**.
2. Enter a **Title**, **Price**, and **Location**. These are required.
3. Choose a **Condition**, **Category**, and **Delivery Method**.
4. Add up to seven photos.
5. Write a **Description**.
6. Select **List Item**.

Your listing appears in **My Listings**, which you can open with the package icon in the Marketplace header. From there you can add a new listing, edit an existing one, or remove it.

## 11. Your profile

Open **Profile** from the bottom navigation to see your profile.

At the top you can see:

- Your profile photo, display name, and username.
- Your **Posts**, **Followers**, and **Following** counts.
- Your posts as a grid.
- A **Saved** tab with posts you bookmarked.

Select **Followers** or **Following** to open the full list.

### Edit your profile

1. Select **Edit Profile**, or choose **Edit Profile** from the three-dot menu.
2. Change your display name or username.
3. Select the camera button to upload a new profile photo.
4. Select **save**.

You can select **Remove photo** to go back to an initials-based avatar.

Usernames must be unique and can contain only letters, numbers, dots, and underscores.

### View a post

Select any post in your grid to open it. From the post view you can:

- Like the post.
- Read and add comments.
- Save or unsave the post.
- Edit or delete your own post using the three-dot menu.

### View someone else's profile

Open a profile from Search, a post, or a conversation. From another person's profile you can:

- View their posts and follower counts.
- Select **Message** to start a conversation.
- Report the account from the three-dot menu.

### Account options

The three-dot menu on your own profile also includes **Request Delete Account**. This demo shows a confirmation request; it does not permanently delete the account.

## 12. Saved posts

Saved posts let you keep interesting content in one place.

To save a post, tap the **bookmark** icon on:

- A Home feed post
- A Reel
- A post in a Page feed
- A post opened from your profile

The bookmark fills in to show that the post is saved. Tap it again to unsave.

To find saved posts:

1. Open your profile.
2. Select the **Saved** tab.

The Saved tab is private to your own profile.

---

## 13. Appearance

Use the **Light / Dark** button at the top-right of the app window to switch themes.

- **Dark** mode uses dark backgrounds and light text.
- **Light** mode uses light backgrounds and dark text.
- Your choice is remembered the next time you open the app.

The mobile-style app frame stays centered in the browser window on larger screens.

## 14. Quick answers

### Can I sign in without creating an account?

Yes. In this demo, enter any email and password and select **Sign In**.

### Why did my posts, saves, or listings disappear?

This is a front-end demo. Most data is kept in memory and resets when you reload the page. The theme choice is the exception and is remembered.

### What does the red badge on Marketplace mean?

It shows the total quantity of items in your cart. A badge of **9+** means ten or more items.

### What does the red badge on Pages mean?

A red count on the **Requests** tab, or on the member icon of a page card, shows pending join requests for pages you manage. Open **Requests** or the **Manage Team** tab to review them.

### How do I stop following someone?

Open **Search**, find the person, and select **Unfollow**.

### How do I remove a post?

Open the post's three-dot menu on Home or from your profile post view and select **Delete post**.

### How do I change my profile picture?

Open your profile, select **Edit Profile**, select the camera button on your photo, choose an image, and select **save**.

### How do I contact a seller?

Open the listing and select **Message**, or open the seller's profile and select **Message**.

### Where is the Marketplace?

Open **Pages** from the bottom navigation. The Marketplace card is at the top.

### Why can't I see page management options?

Management options depend on your role. Admins and moderators see **Manage Team**, **Insights**, and **Settings**. Members see a smaller set of tabs, and visitors see only public page content.
